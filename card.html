What is CVV/CVC?

CVV (Card Verification Value) or CVC (Card Verification Code) is a security code used to verify that you have the physical card during online transactions.

• Visa, Mastercard, Discover: 3-digit code on the back of your card (right side of signature strip)
• American Express: 4-digit code on the front of your card (above the card number)
